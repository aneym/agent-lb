## Why

The All account list should make accounts closest to exhaustion easy to find across providers.

## What Changes

- Default the All view to remaining usage, lowest first, independently of provider-view sort preferences.
- Compare weekly percentages first, falling back to monthly then five-hour percentages when unavailable.
- Keep unknown telemetry last and deterministic name/id tie-breaking.

## Impact

Native macOS presentation only; no account data, routing, reserves, or quota changes.
