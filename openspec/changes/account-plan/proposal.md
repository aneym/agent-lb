# Account plan

## Why
Live pool capacity and E16 trial costs should inform account purchases without changing routing or subscriptions.

## What Changes
- Expose weekly refill cohorts and account counts on pools.
- Compute Budget, Balanced and Unlimited plans from the installed interim ladder and trial costs.
- Add `route plan` and show refill cohorts in `route menu`.

## Impact
Pools API and routing CLI; recommendations only, no account mutations.
